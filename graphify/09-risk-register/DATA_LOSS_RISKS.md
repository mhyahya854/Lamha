# Data-loss risks

Highest risks are partial cross-drive moves, disk-full/disconnect/crash, sidecar corruption/future versions, authority conflicts, companion-set partial state, wrong merge/split, Trash/permanent-delete confusion, overlay reconciliation, and SQLite being treated as authority. Prevention is prepare/stage/verify/commit durability, complete-bundle operations, preserved exact bytes/values, transparent operation history, backups, Review escalation, and disposable-copy failure injection.
